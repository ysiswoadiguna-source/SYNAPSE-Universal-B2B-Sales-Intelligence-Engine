# Signal dictionary

Read this when a customer says something ambiguous. Each phrase lists what it may mean, what would tell the readings apart, and the move. None of these phrases has one fixed meaning — the dictionary gives hypotheses to test, not conclusions. Report them to the user as possibilities, with the question that separates them.

5. Behaviour (what they do, not what they say)

## 1. Timing and delay
| They say | May mean | What separates the readings | Move |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Harusnya bulan ini" | A real plan with soft timing · hope without approval | Whether a PR, budget line, or project date exists | Wait; one touch near the date |
| "Nanti kami kabari" | Low priority · waiting on someone · a polite no | Whether they will name a decision window | Ask once for the window, then wait |
| "Masih proses internal" | Approval in progress · stalled with no owner | Who it is with and what they need | Ask what would help the approver decide |
| "Ditunda dulu" | Budget moved · project postponed · need gone | Whether a review date exists | Ask for the review month; park with a date |
| "Urgent, butuh cepat" | A real breakdown or deadline · pressure tactic for price or priority | Whether they answer spec questions as fast as they ask | Answer fast; ask the required on-site date |

## 2. Price and budget
| They say | May mean | What separates the readings | Move |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Sudah best price?" | Routine check · real gap to budget | Whether a figure or competitor follows | Hold: "sudah nett"; listen for what comes next |
| "Sebelah lebih murah" | A comparable offer · a different scope · a bluff | Whether they will share what the other offer includes | Ask for scope; compare like for like |
| "Samakan harga bulan lalu, PO hari ini" | Strong intent with a condition | Whether stock and old price can still be honoured | Verify with the user first; then answer |
| "Budget kami cuma segini" | A true ceiling · an opening position | Whether they engage with a reduced-scope option | Offer a scope that fits; note what is left out |
| "Fokus penyesuaian spec dulu" | Scope still open · avoiding the budget topic | Whether the spec changes reduce cost or just shift | Ask what the change needs to achieve |
| "Minta penawaran resmi" (no spec) | Collecting prices · needs a document for approval | Whether they can provide spec and a date | Indicative info; ask for spec before a formal quote |

## 3. Authority and process
| They say | May mean | What separates the readings | Move |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Masih diajukan ke manajemen" | Real approval step · they are not the decider and are unsure | Whether they know when management meets | Ask once for a decision window |
| "Saya teruskan ke user" | A gatekeeper doing their job · no influence | Whether technical questions come back | Offer to coordinate directly "for the spec" |
| "Atasan minta pembanding" | Formal three-quote rule · we are the comparison | Whether we had contact before the request | Give a correct offer; scale effort to the evidence |
| "Vendor harus terdaftar dulu" | A real process step, and a good sign | Whether they send the registration forms | Complete it promptly; it is a commitment signal |
| "Bisa kirim company profile?" | Early screening · due diligence before PO | Stage of the deal | Send; ask what the evaluation looks at |

## 4. Interest and intent
| They say | May mean | What separates the readings | Move |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Menarik, nanti kita lihat" | Polite interest · no current need | Whether a problem or date is mentioned | Ask what would make it relevant; nurture |
| "Boleh, kirim saja penawarannya" | Convenience · a way to end the conversation | Whether they answer one qualifying question | Ask the one detail the offer depends on |
| "Stoknya ready?" | Near decision · checking options | Whether quantity and date follow | Answer only with verified stock status |
| "Bisa kirim ke [lokasi]?" | Logistics planning — usually late-stage | Whether a receiving contact is offered | Confirm; ask receiving details |
| "Garansinya bagaimana?" | Risk check before commitment · comparing vendors | Who is asking — user or purchasing | Answer with the user's confirmed terms only |

## 5. Behaviour
Actions are stronger evidence than words. Weigh them accordingly.

| They do | Reading |
|---|---|
| Send drawings, photos, or data unasked | Real need; they want an accurate offer |
| Bring another person into the thread | The decision is moving inside their organisation |
| Ask about invoices, tax documents, delivery notes, vendor forms | Preparing to buy |
| Reply quickly to technical questions, slowly to price | The user is engaged; price sits with someone else |
| Reply only when price is mentioned | Price collection or a comparison quote |
| Read messages without replying, repeatedly | Not a priority now; another message will not change that |
| Ask for a re-quote with no change in scope | Budget gap, or leverage against another vendor |
| Let their own stated date pass silently | The plan slipped; ask once what changed |
| Go quiet immediately after receiving the price | Price shock, internal review, or the quote was all they needed |

When words and behaviour disagree, trust behaviour, and say so to the user.
